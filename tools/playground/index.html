Rein-NPC playground

Three characters, one 4B model running in your browser. The model writes the lines and decides when a tool is needed, when a name is being rewritten, when a fact of the world is under attack. Under every reply you see its decisions and what the game did with them.

Click a character on the map, or one of the buttons, to talk. They wander about on their own.

Everything runs on your machine through wllama (llama.cpp in WebAssembly and WebGPU). Nothing you type leaves the browser. Model: Rein-NPC-4B, Apache-2.0. Pixel art: Zelda-like tilesets and sprites by ArMM1998, CC0.
